01 / AI · FINTECH · RAG

FinRAG AI

Financial Research Assistant

A full-stack AI-powered platform designed to help users analyze financial documents such as annual reports, earnings reports, investor presentations and research papers using Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

Problem

Financial documents contain large amounts of information, making it difficult to quickly locate relevant evidence, compare companies and extract useful insights.

Approach

Built an AI-assisted document research workflow combining document retrieval, semantic search and language models to provide context-aware responses and research-oriented outputs.

Key Features

Document Chat + Citations Financial Insights Risk Analysis Company Comparison Semantic Search Research Reports Analytics Dashboard

Technology

React · TypeScript · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · ChromaDB · LangChain · OpenAI · Docker · GitHub Actions

What I Learned

RAG pipeline design, vector databases, AI system design, financial data interpretation, full-stack development, cloud deployment and challenges around information accuracy and document processing.

03 / RAG · RESEARCH · AI

Bric.Re

AI-Powered Research Assistant

An AI research assistant created to help users find, understand and explore information through evidence-based insights.

The Problem

Research can quickly become overwhelming. A single topic can lead to many articles, videos and sources, making it difficult to identify information that is actually relevant and trustworthy.

The Challenge

While building Bric.Re, I encountered challenges with retrieving relevant information, reducing inaccurate responses and making the output genuinely useful rather than simply convincing.

What I Learned

Building AI systems is not only about using a model. It also involves understanding the problem, testing assumptions, learning from failures and continuously improving the solution.
